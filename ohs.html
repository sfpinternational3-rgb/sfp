Occupational Health & Safety Course

Occupational health and safety management focuses on identifying workplace hazards, assessing occupational risks, preventing work-related injuries and ill health, and creating safer working environments.

This course introduces learners to the principles of Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems (OHSMS), using ISO 45001:2018 as the main reference. It explores hazard identification, risk assessment, worker participation, operational controls, incident investigation, and continual improvement.

Learners can develop an understanding of how a structured safety management system helps organizations manage occupational health and safety risks and improve their workplace safety practices.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand occupational health and safety management principles.
  • Identify workplace hazards and understand risk assessment.
  • Understand the structure and purpose of ISO 45001:2018.
  • Explore operational controls and workplace safety procedures.
  • Understand incident reporting, investigation, and corrective action.
  • Learn how monitoring and continual improvement support safer workplaces.

Who Is This Course For?

This subject may be useful for occupational health and safety officers, supervisors, managers, team leaders, workplace safety representatives, operational personnel, and individuals interested in occupational safety. Contact the institute to confirm the entry requirements for the specific training programme.

Why Is Occupational Health and Safety Important?

Effective occupational health and safety management helps organizations recognize hazards, control risks, improve worker participation, and strengthen workplace safety practices. A systematic approach also supports the identification of opportunities to improve safety performance over time.

  • Continual improvement of the safety management system

Understanding ISO 45001 Certification

ISO 45001 specifies requirements for an occupational health and safety management system. Completing a training course does not, by itself, certify an individual's professional competence or certify an organization's management system.

Before enrolling, ask about the course certificate, any external examination, assessment criteria, and any additional certification fees.
