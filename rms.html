Risk Management System Course

A Risk Management System provides organizations with a structured and consistent approach to identifying, analysing, evaluating, treating, and monitoring risks that may affect their objectives. An effective system helps decision-makers understand uncertainty, take advantage of opportunities, and reduce the likelihood and consequences of unwanted events.

This course introduces learners to the principles of risk management and the guidance provided by ISO 31000 for establishing and maintaining a risk management framework and process. It explores risk context, identification, analysis, evaluation, treatment, communication, monitoring, and review.

Learners can develop an understanding of how organizations set risk criteria, build risk registers, prioritize risks, select suitable treatment options, and report on risk performance. The course also introduces the importance of leadership, risk culture, stakeholder communication, and integrating risk management into planning and decision-making.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand the principles and purpose of risk management.
  • Understand the principles, framework, and process described in ISO 31000.
  • Identify risks, sources of risk, and opportunities.
  • Analyse and evaluate risks using likelihood, consequence, and risk criteria.
  • Explore risk treatment options, controls, and contingency planning.
  • Learn about risk registers, monitoring, reporting, and review.
  • Understand how to build a risk-aware culture and support continual improvement.

Who Is This Course For?

This subject may be useful for risk managers, quality managers, compliance officers, internal auditors, project managers, business owners, supervisors, consultants, students, and individuals interested in risk management. It may also be relevant to organizations seeking to understand or improve how they manage risk. Contact the institute to confirm the entry requirements for the specific training programme.

Why Is a Risk Management System Important?

A Risk Management System helps an organization take a systematic approach to uncertainty. Depending on its context and how the system is implemented, it can support better-informed decisions, earlier detection of threats, more efficient use of resources, stronger governance, improved stakeholder confidence, and greater organizational resilience.

Risk Management System Course Syllabus

The following is a proposed learning outline covering core Risk Management topics. The official syllabus, course depth, and assessment arrangements should be confirmed with the institute before this outline is presented as the final curriculum.

Module 1: Introduction to Risk Management
  • Meaning of risk, uncertainty, and objectives
  • Purpose and value of risk management
  • Benefits of a Risk Management System
  • Roles and responsibilities in risk management
Module 2: Introduction to ISO 31000
  • Purpose and scope of ISO 31000
  • Principles of effective risk management
  • The risk management framework
  • Leadership, commitment, and integration into governance
Module 3: Context and Risk Identification
  • Establishing internal and external context
  • Defining scope, objectives, and risk criteria
  • Techniques for identifying risks and opportunities
  • Building and maintaining a risk register
Module 4: Risk Analysis and Evaluation
  • Assessing likelihood and consequence
  • Qualitative, semi-quantitative, and quantitative approaches
  • Risk matrices and risk ratings
  • Comparing results with risk criteria and prioritizing risks
Module 5: Risk Treatment and Controls
  • Risk treatment options: avoid, reduce, share, accept
  • Selecting and evaluating controls
  • Treatment plans, responsibilities, and timelines
  • Residual risk and risk appetite
  • Risk considerations in projects and outsourced activities
Module 6: Business Continuity and Crisis Preparedness
  • Identifying critical activities and potential disruptions
  • Contingency and continuity planning
  • Responding to incidents and crises
  • Testing and reviewing response arrangements
Module 7: Communication, Monitoring, and Review
  • Communication and consultation with stakeholders
  • Monitoring risks and the effectiveness of controls
  • Risk reporting and key risk indicators
  • Recording and reporting risk information
Module 8: Risk Culture and Continual Improvement
  • Building a risk-aware organizational culture
  • Reviewing the risk management framework
  • Learning from incidents and near misses
  • Continual improvement of risk management

ISO 31000 Learning and Implementation

Training may help learners understand the guidance in ISO 31000 and how a risk management framework and process can be implemented and maintained. Confirm with the institute whether the programme covers awareness, implementation, risk assessment practice, or another specific course level.

ISO 31000 provides guidelines rather than requirements, so organizations are not certified against it in the way they are against management system standards. Completing a training course does not, by itself, certify an organization's risk management practices.
