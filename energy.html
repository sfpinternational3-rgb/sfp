Energy Management System Course

An Energy Management System provides organizations with a structured approach to understanding how they use energy, setting targets for improvement, and managing energy performance over time. An effective system can help organizations reduce energy consumption and cost while supporting their operational and environmental objectives.

This course introduces learners to the principles of energy management and the role of ISO 50001 in establishing and maintaining an Energy Management System (EnMS). It explores energy reviews, significant energy uses, energy baselines and performance indicators, objectives and action plans, operational control, performance evaluation, and continual improvement.

Learners can develop an understanding of how organizations identify where and how energy is used, find opportunities to improve energy efficiency, plan and monitor improvement actions, and evaluate results. The course also introduces the importance of leadership, employee awareness, procurement and design considerations, and documented information.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand the principles and purpose of energy management.
  • Understand the structure and requirements of ISO 50001.
  • Conduct an energy review and identify significant energy uses.
  • Understand energy baselines and energy performance indicators.
  • Explore energy objectives, action plans, and operational controls.
  • Learn about monitoring, measurement, and performance evaluation.
  • Understand corrective action and continual improvement of energy performance.

Who Is This Course For?

This subject may be useful for energy managers, facilities and maintenance managers, engineers, sustainability officers, quality and environmental managers, internal auditors, consultants, students, and individuals interested in energy management. It may also be relevant to organizations seeking to understand or improve their energy use. Contact the institute to confirm the entry requirements for the specific training programme.

Why Is an Energy Management System Important?

An Energy Management System helps an organization take a systematic approach to its energy use. Depending on its context and how the system is implemented, it can support lower energy costs, reduced consumption and emissions, better visibility of energy performance, improved planning of equipment and processes, and stronger awareness among staff.

Energy Management System Course Syllabus

The following is a proposed learning outline covering core Energy Management System topics. The official syllabus, course depth, and assessment arrangements should be confirmed with the institute before this outline is presented as the final curriculum.

Module 1: Introduction to Energy Management
  • Meaning and purpose of energy management
  • Energy sources, use, and efficiency
  • Benefits of an Energy Management System
  • Roles and responsibilities in energy management
Module 2: Introduction to ISO 50001
  • Purpose, scope, and application of ISO 50001
  • Structure and main elements of the standard
  • Understanding organizational context
  • Leadership, energy policy, and responsibilities
Module 3: Energy Review and Significant Energy Uses
  • Analysing energy use and consumption
  • Identifying significant energy uses
  • Identifying variables that affect energy performance
  • Identifying opportunities for improving energy performance
Module 4: Baselines and Energy Performance Indicators
  • Establishing energy baselines
  • Selecting energy performance indicators
  • Normalizing data for relevant variables
  • Planning for energy data collection
Module 5: Planning and Operational Control
  • Establishing energy objectives and action plans
  • Operational planning and control of significant energy uses
  • Maintenance and efficient operation of equipment
  • Energy considerations in design and procurement
  • Raising energy awareness and competence
Module 6: Energy Efficiency Opportunities
  • Efficiency of lighting, heating, cooling, and ventilation
  • Motors, pumps, compressed air, and process equipment
  • Renewable energy and alternative sources
  • Evaluating costs, savings, and payback of improvements
Module 7: Energy Performance Evaluation
  • Monitoring, measurement, and analysis of energy performance
  • Evaluating compliance with legal and other requirements
  • Internal audits and management review
  • Documented information and record control
Module 8: Nonconformity and Continual Improvement
  • Identifying nonconformities in the system
  • Corrective action and root-cause analysis
  • Reviewing the Energy Management System
  • Continual improvement of energy performance

ISO 50001 Learning and Implementation

Training may help learners understand ISO 50001 requirements and how an Energy Management System can be implemented and maintained. Confirm with the institute whether the programme covers awareness, implementation, internal auditing, or another specific course level.

Completing a training course does not, by itself, certify an organization's Energy Management System to ISO 50001. Organizational certification involves a separate conformity assessment conducted through an appropriate certification process.
