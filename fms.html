Food Safety Management System Course

A Food Safety Management System provides organizations in the food chain with a structured approach to identifying, controlling, and monitoring hazards that could make food unsafe for consumers. An effective system helps organizations protect public health, meet customer and regulatory expectations, and maintain confidence in the food they produce, handle, or supply.

This course introduces learners to the principles of food safety management and the role of ISO 22000 in establishing and maintaining a Food Safety Management System (FSMS). It explores hazard analysis, HACCP principles, prerequisite programmes, operational controls, traceability, emergency preparedness, verification, and continual improvement.

Learners can develop an understanding of how organizations identify biological, chemical, and physical hazards, determine critical control points, set monitoring and corrective action arrangements, and verify that controls are effective. The course also introduces the importance of leadership, communication along the food chain, employee competence, and documented information.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand the principles and purpose of food safety management.
  • Understand the structure and requirements of ISO 22000.
  • Identify biological, chemical, and physical food safety hazards.
  • Understand HACCP principles and the role of critical control points.
  • Explore prerequisite programmes, hygiene practices, and operational controls.
  • Learn about traceability, recall, and emergency preparedness.
  • Understand verification, corrective action, and continual improvement of food safety.

Who Is This Course For?

This subject may be useful for food safety officers, quality assurance and quality control staff, production and kitchen supervisors, hospitality and catering managers, food handlers, internal auditors, consultants, students, and individuals interested in food safety. It may also be relevant to organizations seeking to understand or improve their food safety practices. Contact the institute to confirm the entry requirements for the specific training programme.

Why Is a Food Safety Management System Important?

A Food Safety Management System helps an organization take a systematic and preventive approach to food safety. Depending on its context and how the system is implemented, it can support the protection of consumers, awareness of regulatory requirements, reduced risk of contamination and recalls, more consistent hygiene practices, and greater trust from customers and partners.

Food Safety Management System Course Syllabus

The following is a proposed learning outline covering core Food Safety Management System topics. The official syllabus, course depth, and assessment arrangements should be confirmed with the institute before this outline is presented as the final curriculum.

Module 1: Introduction to Food Safety Management
  • Meaning and purpose of food safety management
  • Foodborne illness and public health
  • Benefits of a Food Safety Management System
  • Roles and responsibilities in food safety
Module 2: Introduction to ISO 22000
  • Purpose, scope, and application of ISO 22000
  • Structure and main elements of the standard
  • Understanding organizational context and the food chain
  • Leadership, food safety policy, and responsibilities
Module 3: Food Safety Hazards
  • Biological hazards: bacteria, viruses, and parasites
  • Chemical hazards, including allergens and residues
  • Physical hazards and foreign matter
  • Cross-contamination and its prevention
Module 4: Prerequisite Programmes and Hygiene
  • Good hygiene and good manufacturing practices
  • Cleaning, sanitation, and pest control
  • Personal hygiene, health, and staff training
  • Premises, equipment, water, and waste management
Module 5: HACCP and Hazard Control
  • Preparing for hazard analysis: product and process description
  • Conducting a hazard analysis
  • Determining critical control points and critical limits
  • Monitoring, corrective actions, and record keeping
  • Controlling hazards from suppliers and outsourced processes
Module 6: Traceability, Recall, and Emergency Preparedness
  • Traceability systems for products and ingredients
  • Handling nonconforming and potentially unsafe products
  • Withdrawal and recall procedures
  • Preparing for and responding to food safety emergencies
Module 7: Verification and Performance Evaluation
  • Verification and validation of control measures
  • Monitoring and measuring food safety performance
  • Internal audits and management review
  • Documented information and record control
Module 8: Nonconformity and Continual Improvement
  • Identifying nonconformities in the system
  • Corrective action and root-cause analysis
  • Updating the Food Safety Management System
  • Continual improvement of food safety performance

ISO 22000 and HACCP Learning and Implementation

Training may help learners understand ISO 22000 requirements, HACCP principles, and how a Food Safety Management System can be implemented and maintained. Confirm with the institute whether the programme covers awareness, implementation, internal auditing, or another specific course level.

Completing a training course does not, by itself, certify an organization's Food Safety Management System to ISO 22000. Organizational certification involves a separate conformity assessment conducted through an appropriate certification process.
