Quality Systems Management Course

Quality Systems Management focuses on how organizations establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the processes used to deliver products and services that meet customer and applicable requirements.

This course introduces learners to quality management principles and the role of ISO 9001 in developing a structured Quality Management System (QMS). It explores process management, documented information, performance evaluation, corrective action, and continual improvement.

Learners can develop an understanding of how effective quality management supports customer satisfaction, consistency, accountability, and better organizational performance.

What You Will Learn

  • Understand quality management principles.
  • Understand the purpose and structure of ISO 9001.
  • Identify processes and their interactions.
  • Understand quality objectives and performance monitoring.
  • Learn about documented information and process control.
  • Explore corrective action and continual improvement.

Who Is This Course For?

The subject may be useful for quality professionals, supervisors, managers, process owners, internal auditors, students, and individuals interested in quality management. Contact the institute to confirm the entry requirements for the specific training programme.

Course Syllabus

The following is a proposed learning outline for the Quality Systems Management course. Confirm the official syllabus with the institute before publishing it as the final curriculum.

Module 1: Introduction to Quality Management
  • Meaning and importance of quality
  • Quality management principles
  • Benefits of a Quality Management System
Module 2: Introduction to ISO 9001
  • Purpose and scope of ISO 9001
  • Structure of the standard
  • Understanding organizational requirements
Module 3: Processes and Documentation
  • Process approach and process interactions
  • Documented information
  • Document and record control
Module 4: Risk and Performance
  • Risk-based thinking
  • Quality objectives and monitoring
  • Evaluation of process performance
Module 5: Improvement and Corrective Action
  • Identifying nonconformities
  • Corrective action and root-cause analysis
  • Continual improvement
